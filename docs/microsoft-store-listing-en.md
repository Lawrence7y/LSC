## 完整描述 / Description

```
Live Stream Clipper (LSC) is a multi-room live recording and clipping tool built for stream highlighters, esports fans and content creators.

RECORD UP TO 12 ROOMS AT ONCE
• Add Douyin, Bilibili, Huya, Kuaishou, Douyu, Xiaohongshu, Weibo or direct stream links
• Up to 12 concurrent recordings and 4 low-latency previews
• Hardware encoding (NVIDIA NVENC / Intel QSV / AMD AMF) or CPU encoding, plus lossless stream copy mode

CLIP IN ONE CLICK
• Watch synchronized multi-room previews on one screen
• Press I / O (or click) to mark clip in/out points on the shared timeline
• Add clips, review boundaries, then export — vertical 9:16 or horizontal

MULTI-ANGLE SYNCHRONIZED EXPORT
• One-click audio alignment across rooms using audio cross-correlation
• Export multiple camera angles of the same moment perfectly in sync

AI HIGHLIGHT DETECTION (VALORANT)
• Continuous analysis auto-detects combat rounds from the recording
• OCR-based round detection with round-by-round review and confirm workflow
• Post-recording final scan to capture trailing rounds

FAST EXPORT WORKFLOW
• Global export queue with progress and one-click cancel
• Optional JianYing (CapCut China) draft generation for further editing
• Open exported files or their folders directly after export

Stable, private and local: all recording, analysis and export runs on your own PC.
```
